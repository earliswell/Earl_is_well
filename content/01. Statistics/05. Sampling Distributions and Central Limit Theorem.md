## Introduction

**DIFINITION 7.1**
- A Statistic is a function of the observable random variables in a sample and known constants.
- 다시 말해 우리는 random하게 관측된 샘플을 Statistic이라고 부른다.
- 예를 들어 $\bar{y} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i y_i$에서 $\bar{y}$를 statistic이라고 부른다. 이를 제외하고도 sample median, sample variance 또한 statistic에 속한다.
- Statistics은 sample로 부터 알려지지 않은 population(모집단)을 잘 추론하기 위해 사용된다. 
	- 이에 따라 Statistic은 확률 분포를 갖고 우리는 이를 **Sample Distributions**라고 부른다.

## Sampling Distributions Related to the Normal Distribution
- 우리가 흔히 알고 있는 Normal Distribution을 통해서 공부를 해보자.
	- $Y \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$

**THEOREM 7.1**
- Let $Y_1, Y_2, \dots, Y_n$ be a random sample of size n from a normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. Then$$\bar{Y} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_iY_i$$is normally distributed with mean $\mu_{\bar{Y}} = \mu$ and variance $\sigma^2_{\bar{Y}} = \sigma^2$
**Proof**$$\begin{align}\bar{Y} &= \frac{1}{n}\sum_iY_i = \frac{1}{n}(Y_1)+\frac{1}{n}(Y_2)+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}(Y_n) \\ &=a_1Y_1+a_2Y_2+\cdots+a_nY_n, \qquad \text{where} \, a_i =1/n, i=1,2,\dots,n \end{align}$$
- $\bar{Y}$ is a linear combination of $Y_1,Y_2, \dots, Y_n$, and Theorem 6.3 can be applied to conclude that $\bar{Y}$ is normally distributed with $$E(\bar{Y}) = E\left[\frac{1}{n}(Y_1)+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}(Y_n)\right] = \frac{1}{n}(\mu) + \cdots+\frac{1}{n}(\mu) = \frac{1}{n}\times n\mu = \mu$$and$$V(\bar{Y}) = V\left[\frac{1}{n}(Y_1) + \cdots+\frac{1}{n}(Y_n)\right] = \frac{1}{n^2}(\sigma^2+\cdots + \sigma^2) = \frac{1}{n^2}\times n\sigma^2 = \frac{1}{n}\sigma^2$$
**THEOREM 7.2**
- Let $Y_1, Y_2, \dots, Y_n$ be defined as in Theorem 7.1 Then $Z_i = (Y_i - \mu) / \sigma$ are independent, standard normal random variables, $i=1,2, \dots, n$ and $$\sum_iZ_i^2 = \sum_i\left(\frac{Y_i-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2$$ has a $\chi^2$ distribution with n degrees of freedom (df).
**Proof**
- The fact that $\sum_i Z_i^2$ has a $\chi^2$ distribution with n df follows directly from Theorem 6.4.
**Characteristic**
- 항상 양의 값을 갖으며, Non-symmetric(비대칭)의 모양을 갖는다. 
- $P(\chi^2 > \chi_\alpha^2) = \alpha$![[Pasted image 20241128144613.png]]
- 우리는 Random Sample $Y_1, Y_2, \dots, Y_n$에서 Sample Variance로 Population variance를 추정해야할 때 $\chi^2$ distribution이 유용하게 사용된다. 
- Sample Variance$$S^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_i(Y_i-\bar{Y})^2$$
**THEOREM 7.3**
- Let $Y_1, Y_2, \dots, Y_n$ be a random sample from a normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. Then$$\frac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2} = \frac{1}{\sigma^2}\sum_i(Y_i-\bar{Y})^2$$has a $\chi^2$ distribution with (n-1) df. Also, $\bar{y}$ and $S^2$ are independent random variables.
