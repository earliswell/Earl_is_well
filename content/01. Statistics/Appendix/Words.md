### DEFINITION 2.13
Let $N$ and $n$ represent the numbers of elements in the population and sample, respectively. If the sampling is conducted in such a way that each of the $\begin{pmatrix} N \\ n \end{pmatrix}$ samples has an equal probability of being selected, the sampling is said to be random, and the result is said to be a random sample.
